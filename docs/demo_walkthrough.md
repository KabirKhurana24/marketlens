# 2-Minute Demo Walkthrough

Use this order in a call or screen share. Keep the synthetic-data disclaimer visible.

## 0:00–0:20 — Frame the problem

“I chose a common marketplace product trap: teams celebrate more leads, but the real user outcome depends on whether those leads are relevant and whether someone responds. I wanted one prototype that connects demand, supply quality and fulfilment.”

## 0:20–0:55 — Show Overview

Start with all markets and the 30-day window. Walk through the search → listing view → lead → qualified lead funnel. Point out the difference between raw leads and qualified leads. Change the market filter to Cairo, then Riyadh, and show how a blended average hides different problems.

## 0:55–1:20 — Show Market breakdown

Compare the five markets. Explain the three demo hypotheses:

- Cairo: lead quality and response time deserve investigation.
- Riyadh: discovery is strong, but detail-to-lead conversion is weaker.
- Dubai: a low stale share still represents a large number of records at marketplace scale.

Do not present these as real market findings; they are generated demo patterns.

## 1:20–1:40 — Show Listing health

Filter to “Needs attention.” Explain the score weights and show one example where stale inventory, low photo coverage, missing verification or price deviation reduces the score. Flag one record to show the review-queue interaction.

## 1:40–2:00 — Show Experiment lab

Choose a market, change the assumed lift and show how the scenario changes. Explain that the simulator is not a forecast. Name the primary metric and guardrails, then say what evidence would make you ship or stop.

## Close with the limitation

“The data is synthetic and weekly aggregate, so I can surface hypotheses but cannot claim causality. With real access, my first step would be to validate event definitions, inspect market/device/purpose cohorts, and run a powered experiment in one market before scaling.”
